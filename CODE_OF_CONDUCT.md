# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want MusicPlayer to be a welcoming and respectful open-source project for everyone.

Whether you are submitting code, opening an issue, reviewing a pull request, asking a question, or participating in a discussion, please help keep the community friendly, constructive, and inclusive.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors and participants are expected to:

* Be respectful and considerate of others.
* Give constructive feedback and accept feedback respectfully.
* Focus criticism on ideas, code, or decisions rather than individuals.
* Use welcoming and inclusive language.
* Respect different levels of experience and technical backgrounds.
* Keep discussions relevant to the project.
* Respect the privacy and personal boundaries of other participants.
* Follow GitHub's Terms of Service and applicable project policies.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
* Discrimination based on personal characteristics or identity.
* Sexualized language, unwanted sexual attention, or inappropriate content.
* Deliberate trolling, insulting, or repeatedly disrupting discussions.
* Publishing someone's private or personal information without permission.
* Spam, malicious content, or intentionally harmful contributions.
* Retaliating against someone who reports a concern in good faith.
* Any other behavior that would reasonably make the project unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and interactions related to MusicPlayer, including:

* GitHub Issues
* Pull requests
* Discussions
* Code reviews
* Project-related communication
* Other public or private interactions when someone is representing or participating in the MusicPlayer community

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately rather than starting a public discussion about the incident.

You can contact the maintainer through the private contact form available on Ava's portfolio:

**Portfolio:** [https://cozyfolio.vercel.app/](https://cozyfolio.vercel.app/)

Please include enough information to understand what happened, such as relevant links, screenshots, or a brief description of the situation. Only share information that is appropriate and necessary.

Reports made in good faith will be taken seriously and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

The project maintainer may review reports, determine whether the Code of Conduct was violated, and take appropriate action.

Possible responses may include:

* Asking participants to stop inappropriate behavior.
* Removing or editing inappropriate comments where appropriate.
* Closing discussions, issues, or pull requests.
* Temporarily or permanently restricting participation in the project.
* Reporting serious or unlawful behavior to the appropriate platform or authorities when necessary.

The response will depend on the circumstances and severity of the situation.

## Good-Faith Participation

Mistakes happen, especially in technical discussions. Not every disagreement or mistake is a Code of Conduct violation.

We encourage participants to resolve ordinary misunderstandings respectfully and give others an opportunity to correct mistakes.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take action when behavior violates this Code of Conduct, even if the behavior is not explicitly listed above.

The goal of enforcement is to protect a constructive environment while allowing people to learn, contribute, and collaborate.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in the MusicPlayer project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help maintain a respectful and welcoming community.
